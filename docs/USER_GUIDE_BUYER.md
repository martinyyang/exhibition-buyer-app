# User Guide for On-Site Buyers

This guide explains how to use the Exhibition Buyer Collaboration app as an **on-site buyer** at exhibitions.

---

## Overview

As an on-site buyer, you will:
1. Create or join a team
2. Create exhibition events
3. Add booths for each supplier
4. Take photos of products
5. Mark products with flags for remote team review
6. Make final purchasing decisions based on remote team feedback

---

## Step 1: Registration and Team Setup

### 1.1 Register Your Account

1. Open the app in your mobile browser
2. Tap **"Register"** on the login screen
3. Enter your email and password
4. Tap **"Register Account"**
5. You will be automatically logged in

### 1.2 Create a New Team (First-Time Setup)

**If you are the team leader:**

1. After registration, you'll see the Event Selection screen
2. Tap the **"Switch Team"** button in the team header
3. Select **"Create Team"** from the bottom sheet
4. Enter:
   - **Team Name**: e.g., "Spring 2026 Buying Team"
   - **Team Password**: Create a secure password
5. Tap **"Create"**
6. **Important**: Save the **Invite Code** (6 characters) shown in the success message
7. Share the invite code and password with your remote team members

### 1.3 Join an Existing Team

**If someone else created the team:**

1. Get the **Invite Code** (6 characters) and **Password** from your team leader
2. After registration, tap **"Switch Team"**
3. Select **"Join Team"**
4. Enter the invite code or team name
5. Enter the team password
6. Tap **"Verify & Join"**

---

## Step 2: Create an Exhibition Event

1. On the **Event Selection** screen, tap the **+** button (top right)
2. Enter:
   - **Event Name**: e.g., "Canton Fair 2026 - Phase 1"
   - **Start Date**: Tap to select from calendar
3. Tap **"Create"**
4. The event will be automatically set as your active event

---

## Step 3: Add Booths

### 3.1 Create a Booth

1. The app automatically navigates to the **Booth List** after creating an event
2. Tap the **+** button (top right)
3. The booth number is **auto-generated** (001, 002, 003...)
   - You can edit it if needed (e.g., "A15" for actual booth number)
4. **Optional**: Tap **"Take Cover Photo"** to capture a photo of the booth
   - This helps identify the booth visually
5. Tap **"Create"**

### 3.2 Batch Create Multiple Booths

For multiple booths, repeat the process:
- Booth numbers increment automatically: 001 → 002 → 003
- Cover photos are optional but recommended
- You can edit booth details later by long-pressing the booth card

---

## Step 4: Take Product Photos

### 4.1 Enter Booth Photo Gallery

1. On the **Booth List**, tap a booth card
2. You'll see the **Photo Gallery** for that booth

### 4.2 Take Single Photos

1. Tap the **camera button** (bottom right, floating action button)
2. Select **"Single Photo"**
3. Take a photo of the product(s)
4. The photo is automatically uploaded (optimized as WebP format)
5. Repeat for each product or product group

### 4.3 Batch Upload Photos

1. Tap the **camera button**
2. Select **"Batch Upload"**
3. Select multiple photos from your gallery
4. All photos will be uploaded in sequence

**Tip**: Photos are compressed to WebP format (60-80% smaller) for fast upload even on 3G networks.

---

## Step 5: Mark Products with Flags

### 5.1 Add Product Flags

1. Open a photo in the **Photo Gallery**
2. **Tap anywhere on the photo** where you want to mark a product
3. A **crosshair flag** with a number appears
4. Flags are numbered sequentially: 1, 2, 3, etc.
5. Repeat for each product in the photo

**Notes**:
- Flags snap to a grid for precise positioning
- Zoom and pan the photo for accurate placement
- Each flag represents one product

### 5.2 View Flag Details

1. Flags are automatically synced to your remote team
2. Remote team members can see all flags and add pricing information
3. You can view flag details by tapping the flag number below the photo

---

## Step 6: Monitor Remote Team Input

### 6.1 Check Price Conversions

1. Your remote team will enter:
   - **Seller Price** (RMB): The quoted price from the supplier
   - **Converted Price**: Automatically calculated using your team's formula
   - **Target Price**: Your company's target price for comparison
2. Prices appear in real-time as remote team updates them

### 6.2 Review Remote Decisions

Remote team members can mark products as:
- **Purchased**: Recommended to buy
- **Sold Out**: Product unavailable
- (or leave unmarked for your decision)

---

## Step 7: Make Final Purchasing Decisions

### 7.1 Update Product Status

1. Based on remote team feedback and your on-site assessment:
   - Compare **Seller Price** vs **Target Price**
   - Check **Remote Decision** recommendations
2. Make your final decision:
   - **Purchase**: Buy the product
   - **Sold Out**: Mark as unavailable
   - **Give Up**: Skip this product

### 7.2 Track Progress

1. The **Photo Gallery** shows how many products you've processed
2. Use the **flag counter** to track completion per photo
3. All decisions are synced in real-time with your remote team

---

## Step 8: Manage Multiple Events

### 8.1 Switch Between Events

1. Go back to the **Event Selection** screen (tap back from Booth List → Event List)
2. Tap any event card to switch to it
3. The active event is highlighted in green with a **"Current"** badge

### 8.2 Set an Event as Active

1. **Long-press** an event card
2. Select **"Set as Active Event"**
3. This event becomes your default working event

---

## Tips for Efficient On-Site Buying

### Workflow Optimization

1. **Pre-create booths**: Before visiting suppliers, create placeholder booths (001, 002, etc.)
2. **Batch photo capture**: Take multiple photos per booth before moving to the next
3. **Mark immediately**: Add flags right after taking photos while products are fresh in memory
4. **Check remote feedback**: Review prices during breaks between booths

### Connectivity Tips

1. **3G/4G friendly**: Photos are compressed automatically (WebP format)
2. **Offline resilience**: Changes sync automatically when connection restores
3. **Background upload**: Booth covers upload in background, don't wait

### Team Collaboration

1. **Share invite code**: Keep your team invite code and password in a secure note
2. **Real-time sync**: Your remote team sees updates within seconds
3. **Communicate**: Use the online user indicator to see who's currently active

## Troubleshooting

### Can't See Booth/Photo Updates?

- Pull down to refresh the screen
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure you're on the correct active event

### Photo Upload Failed?

- Check internet connection
- Retry by tapping the camera button again
- Photos are retried automatically on reconnection

### Forgot Team Invite Code?

1. Tap **"Switch Team"** button
2. The current team info shows the invite code
3. Tap the **copy icon** to copy it

### Need to Change Team?

1. Tap **"Switch Team"**
2. Select **"Join Team"** to join a different team
3. Enter the new team's invite code and password
